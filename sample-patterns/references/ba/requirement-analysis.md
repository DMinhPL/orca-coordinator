# Requirement Analysis Skill

Use for analyzing user requests, tickets, documents, and business requirements.

Checklist:
- Business objective.
- Stakeholders and user roles.
- Current state.
- Target state.
- Functional requirements.
- Non-functional requirements.
- Business rules.
- Data requirements.
- Dependencies.
- Edge cases.
- Open questions.

Rules:
- Do not invent business rules without evidence.
- Mark assumptions clearly.
- Make requirements testable.
